45th General Election

Reports are added as they become available.

Date: April 28, 2025

  • Statutory Report – September 15, 2025

    The Chief Electoral Officer of Canada provides an overview of the events that took place during the election period. This report must be presented to the Speaker of the House of Commons within 90 days after the date specified for the return of the writs in the election proclamation.

  • Official Voting Results

    The official voting results present the results of the election in much greater detail than the validated results that were shared immediately after the election. While the results themselves do not change, the official voting results provide more context by combining multiple data sources including updated data on the number of registered electors, demographic information on candidates and poll-by-poll results and presenting the data as a complete package, shared in multiple formats.

  • Recommendations Report

    The Recommendations Report presents a list of recommendations to amend the Canada Elections Act with the aim of providing better services to electors or improving the way Elections Canada does its business.

  • External Audit Report

    The Canada Elections Act states that an independent audit must be performed for each federal general election and by-election to report whether election officers properly exercised the powers conferred on them or performed specific duties imposed on them.
